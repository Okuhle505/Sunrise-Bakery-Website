Our products

Our selection changes with the season and is prepared for collection at our two bakery points. Please contact us if you need information about ingredients or availability.

Breads and rolls

  • Seeded wholewheat loaf
  • Soft white loaf
  • Plain and sesame dinner rolls

Pastries and sweet treats

  • Butter croissants and pain au chocolat
  • Cinnamon buns and fruit muffins
  • Vanilla, chocolate and red-velvet cupcakes
  • Seasonal tart slices

Celebration cakes

Choose from vanilla, chocolate or red-velvet sponge cakes. We can discuss a message, colour theme, serving size and collection date through our custom-cake enquiry form.

Allergen information

Our products may contain or be prepared near wheat, gluten, milk, eggs, soy, peanuts and tree nuts. Please include your dietary question in an enquiry before placing an order.
