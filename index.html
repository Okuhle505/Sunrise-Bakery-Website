Frequently asked questions

How much notice do I need for a celebration cake?

Please allow at least three days for a custom cake. Larger or detailed orders may need additional notice.

Can I ask about allergens?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to tell us about your dietary question before placing an order.

Where can I collect my order?

Orders can be collected from Rosebank or Sandton during normal opening hours.
