About Sunrise Bakery

Sunrise Bakery is a fictional independent bakery created for a neighbourhood that values good food, friendly service and simple everyday celebrations. We serve customers from our Rosebank and Sandton collection points.

Our story begins with a small idea: bread and pastries taste better when they are made with care and shared with others. From a weekday breakfast to a birthday cake, we aim to make each visit feel warm and personal.

Our mission

To make dependable, high-quality baked goods accessible to local customers while offering helpful, welcoming service.

Our vision

To become a trusted everyday bakery where local people can find fresh products and celebrate life’s small and important moments.

What matters to us

  • Freshly prepared products and clear information for customers.
  • Respectful, friendly service at every collection point.
  • Careful handling of special requests, including custom celebration cakes.
  • A welcoming space for families, students and busy local workers.
