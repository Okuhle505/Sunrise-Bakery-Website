# Image Requirements and Source Register

## Image-Sourcing Rules

Only use one of the following:

1. An original photograph created by you, with your name and creation date recorded.
2. A CC0/public-domain image with its source URL and rights status recorded.
3. An image from a provider whose licence permits the intended educational website use; record the creator, title/description, page URL, licence URL and access date.

Do not use images copied from Google Images, social media, another bakery’s website, or a search-result preview unless you have verified a suitable licence or received written permission.
