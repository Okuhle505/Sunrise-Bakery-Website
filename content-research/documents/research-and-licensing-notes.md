## Image-Licensing Guidance Consulted

Creative Commons states that public-domain works may be freely used, while CC0 is a rights-waiver tool. The source page and rights status of each item must still be checked before use.

Unsplash and Pexels describe terms allowing free use of their images, but each selected image still needs a source record in this project so it can be cited accurately and its terms can be checked again before submission.

Harvard references:

- Creative Commons 2026, *Public Domain*, viewed 17 September 2026, &lt;https://creativecommons.org/public-domain/&gt;.
- Pexels 2026, *Free Stock Photo & Video License*, viewed 17 September 2026, &lt;https://www.pexels.com/license/&gt;.
- Unsplash 2026, *License*, viewed 17 September 2026, &lt;https://unsplash.com/license&gt;.

## No External Asset Claim

No externally sourced image, logo, icon, font, map, or text has been added to the current Sunrise Bakery website. The HTML image references are filenames only; the files do not yet exist. This avoids representing any unlicensed asset as part of the project.
